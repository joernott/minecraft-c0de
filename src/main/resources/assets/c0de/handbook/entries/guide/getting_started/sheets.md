The floor looks like old computer paper with green and white stripes. Each strip of paper is a **sheet**. A sheet is 32 blocks wide and runs south without end.

Every sheet holds at most one program. The first player who builds on an empty sheet owns it: on a server, only you and the administrators can change your sheet. When you remove the first line, the sheet is free again. North of the corner of each sheet, the floor shows its number, its coordinates and its owner. To give your sheet to another player, right-click that text with the wrench and pick the player. The black area outside the sheets belongs to nobody. You can place trigger blocks there to start your programs.
