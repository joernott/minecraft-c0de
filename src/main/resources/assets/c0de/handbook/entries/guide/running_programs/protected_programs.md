While a process runs, its sheet is locked. Nobody can place or break blocks on it, not even pistons or explosions. The [wrench](^tools/wrench), the [hammer](^tools/hammer) and the [pliers](^tools/pliers) don't work there.

Dialogs of blocks on the sheet open read-only. The [text marker](^tools/text_marker) still works, because it changes nothing. If you try to change a locked sheet, you get a message naming the running processes.
